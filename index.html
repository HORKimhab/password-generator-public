Use this free tool to generate password online for email, social media, banking, business tools, Wi-Fi, and personal accounts. This secure password generator helps you create strong random passwords. A strong password reduces the risk of brute-force attacks, credential stuffing, and weak-password reuse. For best results, generate a long password, keep it unique for each account, and store it in a trusted password manager.

How to Generate a Strong Password

  1. Set the password length. Use at least 16 characters for important accounts.
  2. Turn on symbols, numbers, lowercase, and uppercase characters for maximum strength.
  3. Optionally add your own custom words to make the password easier to remember.
  4. Keep exclude similar and exclude ambiguous characters on if you need to type the password by hand.
  5. Click Generate Password, then Copy it and save it in your password manager.

Password Security Tips

  • Use a different password for every account so one data breach cannot unlock the others.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever it is available.
  • Avoid personal information such as names, birthdays, phone numbers, and pet names.
  • Store passwords in a reputable password manager instead of notes, screenshots, or chat messages.
  • Change a password immediately if a service reports a data breach.

Password Generator FAQ

How does this password generator work?

It creates a random password based on your selected options directly in your browser, including length, symbols, numbers, uppercase letters, lowercase letters, and custom words.

Is this password generator secure?

Yes. The generator runs locally in your browser and uses the cryptographically secure Web Crypto API, so the password creation process stays on your device instead of relying on a remote server.

Do you store or see my passwords?

No. Passwords are never sent to or saved on any server. If you enable "Save My Preference", only your settings are saved in your own browser, not the passwords.

Is this password generator free?

Yes. It is completely free, works on desktop and mobile, and does not require an account.

What is a strong password?

A strong password is long, unique, hard to predict, and uses a balanced mix of characters. Avoid names, dates, short words, and reused passwords.

How long should my password be?

Use at least 12 characters for everyday accounts and 16 to 20 or more characters for email, banking, and other important accounts. Longer passwords are much harder to crack.

Why exclude similar and ambiguous characters?

Characters like i, l, 1, o, and 0 look alike, and some symbols are hard to type on certain keyboards. Excluding them makes passwords easier to read and type without a meaningful loss of strength when the password is long.

Can I add my own words to the password?

Yes. Enable "Include Your Custom Word" and enter comma-separated words, in English, Khmer, or any other language, to create a password that is easier to remember.
